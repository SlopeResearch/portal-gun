# The Portal Gun and Portal Fluid in the "Rick and Morty" Universe: A Lore Summary

Date compiled: 2026-10-27. Tools: WebSearch and WebFetch. More than 20 searches and page loads were performed.

## 0. Methodology, Limitations, and Honest Caveats

**Confidence scale.**
- **canon** — the fact is shown in an episode or confirmed by a transcript or a Wikipedia episode synopsis.
- **semi-canon** — the fact comes from a summary of the fan wiki (Rick and Morty Wiki, fandom.com), from reviews and recaps, or from fan statements. Wikis are written by fans, and some of their conclusions are interpretive. I did not check them directly against frames or transcripts.
- **fanon** — theories and hypotheses of fans or physicists with no basis in an episode.

**On the sources.**
- I read the fandom.com pages through WebFetch, which returns a brief summary rather than the raw text. Some pages returned an error: `Portal_Fluid`, `Bootleg_Portal_Fluid`, `Evil_Morty's_Portal_Gun`. I did not obtain a dedicated article on the portal fluid. The data on it were assembled from the pages `Portal_Gun`, `Portal_Technology`, `Bootleg_Portal_Chemist_Rick`, `Nick` and `Forgetting_Sarick_Mortshall`, from the transcript of The Ricklantis Mixup, and from press articles.
- The wiki page `Portal_Gun` itself marks the section "Properties of the liquid" as having no episode references. All of its claims are given here as **semi-canon/fanon**.
- The name of the fluid differs between summaries: "Quantum Portal Solution" (`Portal_Gun`) and "Quantum Transport Solution" (a summary of a file page). The exact official name is not confirmed.
- Sites such as `ria-compute.csir.co.za`, `revistasdemo.untumbes.edu.pe`, `theparentcue.org` and `rickandmortyseason8/9.it.com` are SEO junk or domain placeholders. I did not use them as sources of facts. One of them does not even contain episode references.
- Tim Andersen's article (Substack) on quantum tunnelling and the Because Science video ("Could A Portal Gun End All Life On Earth?") are about the **portal gun from Valve's game "Portal"**, not Rick's. For R&M they are usable only as an analogy.
- I did not find a Kyle Hill video specifically about R&M.

**Corrections to the wording of the brief.**
1. "Rick: A Mort Well Lived" is **S6E2**, the episode about the VR game (Roy: A Life Well Lived). It contains no information about the portal gun (tvobsessive).
2. Episode S3E7 is **"The Ricklantis Mixup"**. "Tales from the Citadel" is a name used by the wiki. I was unable to confirm that it is a separate official title of the episode.
3. S3E7 **does not show a portal-fluid factory**. It shows a makeshift "drug lab": Chemist Rick brews counterfeit fluid in the den of the Mortytown Locos (transcript). The factories in this episode make Simple Rick's Wafers (Wikipedia).
4. I **could not confirm** the quote "I don't do magic, Morty, I do science" or the title "Full Meta Jackrick". None of the pages I found contains them. Do not use them as facts without checking against the subtitles.
5. The materials I found contain no explicit "portal blockers" and no "portal travel sickness". The `Portal_Technology` page states directly that it describes no separate portal-blocking mechanism.
6. A numerical portal lifetime is not given anywhere (see section 3).

## 7. Extracted Physical Requirements for the Model

Labels: **[canon]** — confirmed by an episode or transcript. **[semi-canon]** — confirmed by a wiki or review, not checked against frames. **[assumption]** — I propose to adopt it for the model; there is no explicit basis in the sources.

1. **The portal is a flat disk (a two-dimensional region), its orientation is arbitrary, no solid surface is needed.** [semi-canon]
2. **The disk is opaque from the outside, and the other side cannot be seen until you pass through. For the model, it must be decided whether the exit is visible from both sides.** [semi-canon] No confirmation of "visible from both sides" was found, so transparency is an assumption.
3. **Passage is two-way while the portal is open.** [semi-canon]
4. **The lifetime is finite and determined by the user; there is no number of seconds in canon.** [semi-canon; the number is an assumption, for example on the order of seconds]
5. **The edge/wall of the disk does not cut the body during ordinary passage, but cuts an object inside which the portal is opened or which is caught at collapse.** [semi-canon from "Mortynight Run"; "does not cut during passage" is an assumption, because the characters pass through calmly]
6. **A paired portal is needed for a definite exit. Two connected disks must not intersect: an intersection or contact causes a catastrophic collapse ("zero-point vacuum", a real case is the death of Nick).** [semi-canon / canon from the Nick scene]
7. **Momentum conservation during passage is an OPEN QUESTION, a separate line of research.** In some scenes momentum is not conserved (a portal damps a fall), in others (clarified by the customer) it is conserved. The model must allow both regimes: for example, conservation in the throat's frame of reference with a controllable "linkage" parameter between entrance and exit, or a dependence on whether the local frames of the two disks coincide. [semi-canon; the contradictory scenes are the subject of a separate study]
8. **Gas does not pass through a gun portal (it does pass through natural wormholes).** Consequence: there is no air exchange between the sides and no pressure-driven flow. [semi-canon from "Mortynight Run"]
9. **The portal size is variable: from a palm to the scale of a building and of the Citadel.** [canon: palm and Citadel]
10. **The portal requires a consumable resource, the portal fluid. A leak or spill creates uncontrolled mini-portals, a shortage switches the gun off.** [canon: S5E9, S5E10/S6E1]
11. **Poor-quality or incorrectly composed fluid makes the portal lethal (disintegration on touch).** [canon: S3E7]
12. **A portal can "work" faultily, and such a state is not local to a single portal: hacking of the guns and the failed "fluid reboot" led to mass deaths or to users being reset to their original dimensions.** [canon: S5E10, S6E1]
13. **The range is limited by the structure of the multiverse: a standard gun operates inside the Central Finite Curve. Going beyond it requires a different technology (the golden portal).** [canon; there is no numerical range]
14. **Self-destruct portal: the collapse of the gun produces a short-lived portal that sucks in nearby objects.** [semi-canon]
15. **Mass is not strictly limited: the Citadel and a ship were teleported through portals. The energy cost is not determined by canon; the model must accept an uncertain energy source as an assumption.** [canon for the Citadel; the ship is semi-canon; the energy is an assumption]

**Not confirmed and requiring separate verification:** the exact lifetime; visibility from both sides; the quotes about "quantum"/"magic"; the difference between green and golden fluid on screen; the existence of portal blockers; "portal travel sickness".
